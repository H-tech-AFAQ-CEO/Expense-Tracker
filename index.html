Summary

Everything you enter in Obsidian Vault is saved in a database on your own device. The developer cannot see it, and the app does not send it anywhere.

  • No sign-up, login, or personal profile.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • No sharing or selling of data, because we never receive any.
  • Works fully without an internet connection.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following information in a private SQLite database inside the app’s storage on your Android device.

InformationWhy it’s stored
TransactionsTitle, amount, category, date, type (income or expense), and optional note that you enter.
AccountsThe account or card name, the last four digits, and the opening balance you choose to add. Full card numbers are never requested.
PreferencesYour monthly spending cap, currency symbol, and the “hide balances” setting.

This information exists only on your device. It is not uploaded, synced, or backed up by us.

What we collect

Nothing.

The app does not collect, transmit, or have access to your personal or financial information. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising ID, and it does not connect to any third-party service.

Features that touch your device

  • Clipboard. “Export CSV” copies your transactions to your clipboard, and “Import CSV” reads text from it. This happens only when you tap those options. Be aware that other apps may be able to read clipboard contents, so clear it after use.
  • Local backup. “Backup database” saves a copy of your data file in the app’s private storage on the same device. “Restore database” reads that copy back.

Your control

  • Edit or delete any transaction or account from within the app.
  • Erase all transactions from Settings.
  • Export your data as CSV whenever you like.
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Because we hold no copy, deleted data cannot be recovered by us.

Because your data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app or losing the phone without your own export will permanently remove it. Export a CSV regularly if you want a safe copy.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps. Because nothing leaves the device, there is no server that could be breached. Your data is as secure as your phone, so we recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device updated.

The “hide balances” option masks amounts on screen for privacy in public places. It is a display setting and not a form of encryption.

Children’s privacy

Obsidian Vault is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information, we do not knowingly gather data from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s behavior changes, for example if a feature ever needs internet access, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above before the change is released. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
